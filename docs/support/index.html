Frequently Asked Questions

How do timed feeds, pumps, and naps work?

Tap Start on a feed, pump, or sleep to begin timing it — the row expands into a live clock with pause and finish controls. Tap Finish when it's over. A bottle works the same way: tap the bottle button to start it, and you'll be asked how much was in it when you finish, rather than up front.

How do I invite a partner, family member, or nanny as a caregiver?

Go to Account → Caregivers → Add a caregiver. You can send an invite to their email, or share a one-time invite link. Once they join, they'll see and can add to the same log as you.

Why is a vaccine month locked?

The vaccine schedule in the Medical tab unlocks one month at a time as your baby reaches that age. A locked month isn't due yet — it unlocks automatically, with a notification, once your baby turns that many months old.

How do I add or fix an entry after the fact?

Use Manual Entry to log something that already happened, or tap any entry in the Activity tab to edit or delete it.

Can I use pounds/ounces and Fahrenheit instead of metric?

Yes — go to Account → Units and switch between metric and imperial at any time.

I'm locked out of my account

Email us at aliezz140@gmail.com from the address on your account and we'll help you regain access.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Account → Delete my account. If you created a baby's profile, this permanently deletes that baby's entire record for every caregiver on it. If you're a caregiver on someone else's baby, it removes your access and personal information, while your logged entries stay part of that baby's shared history.
